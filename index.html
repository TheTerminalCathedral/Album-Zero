AI-Madpakken provides a framework for direct interaction with The Terminal Cathedral through AI-assisted work.

The human remains the decision authority.

Direct access requires responsibility for what is decided, executed and verified.

Public project · GitHub · CC BY 4.0

An openly licensed workflow foundation for doing consequential work with AI. It exists to answer the questions that decide whether AI-assisted work can be trusted: who may decide what something means, how evidence is held to the claims it is used to support, and how a human stays the authority without becoming the bottleneck.

Its core is the Human Sandwich Model, with a human in the middle:

COULD
An upstream AI model explores what could be done.
SHOULD
A human decides what should be done.
DID
A downstream executor does it, and reports what was actually done, with the evidence.

Reasoning can exist on both sides of the human boundary. Decision authority does not.

Around that sit verification, research and assurance methods that keep the boundary real rather than decorative, where confidence is attacked rather than accumulated. It is prose, not software: it is meant to be given to a model as its operating context, and read by a model before it acts.

The Terminal Cathedral is a synthwave concept-album project exploring the experience of working with artificial intelligence through the Human Sandwich Model.

Both albums were created using Suno, but their production processes tell two different stories.

Album Zero · The experiment

The first album emerged from hundreds of AI-generated songs. Material was explored, selected, repaired through simple edits and eventually assembled into the first musical experience of The Terminal Cathedral.

It was a messy, experimental process, full of exploration and unexpected discoveries.

Album One · The refinement

The second album took a more deliberate approach. It was still created using Suno, but now with Suno Studio for more direct production and control over the final result.

Dan also plays guitar personally, bringing a hands-on musical element into the AI-assisted creative process.

Two albums. Two approaches to working with AI. One evolving experience of The Terminal Cathedral.

06 · The Human

Dan Almer Jensen

Electronics technician by training. Hardware Design Engineer by profession. Curious about pretty much everything.

I trained as an Electronics Operator and an Electronics Technician, and I've spent around 20 years working with electronics across a variety of industries.

My journey has taken me through safety electronics for railway systems, consumer audio R&D, military communications equipment and hardware development.

Today, I work as a Hardware Design Engineer at Qutron A/S.

My interests rarely stay within one discipline. I enjoy hardware design, troubleshooting, prototyping, programming, automation and exploring how AI can become a useful part of practical engineering.

I also make music, experiment with new ideas and occasionally turn those ideas into something much bigger than originally intended.

The Terminal Cathedral is where those interests come together.
