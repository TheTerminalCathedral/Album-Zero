What gets recorded?

This website has an optional visitor-statistics script. It only runs if you choose YES · ALLOW STATS in the Registrar's observation request. If you choose NO · THANKS, or make no choice, nothing is recorded or sent, and the website works exactly the same.

Who runs it, and why

The site and its statistics script are made and run by Dan Almer Jensen, as a personal project. The statistics show which pages people visit, which records they open, which links they follow and how long they stay. They are not used for advertising. No advertising network and no commercial analytics product is used; the script sends its messages to a Google service the site owner set up (see "Where the messages go").

What is recorded, if you choose YES

One short message is sent when:

  • a page opens, and when you leave it (with how long the page was visible);
  • you have stayed on a page for 10 seconds;
  • you choose a section, open or close a record, choose a dialogue option in the original Registrar console, or follow a link;
  • a script error occurs on the page (this can include errors caused by browser extensions).

Each message contains:

  • the page's address, path and title (including any extra parameters in the address you arrived with, such as utm_source);
  • the address of the page you came from, as your browser reports it (for links from other websites usually only the website's name);
  • what you chose: the section, record, dialogue step, option label or link (and the address of an external link);
  • the time, and for "leaving the page" the visible time in milliseconds;
  • your device type, only as "desktop", "tablet" or "mobile", worked out in your browser;
  • a random visit number (see below);
  • the error message, if a script error occurred.

The messages do not contain your name, an email address, an account, or your browser's identification string.

The visit number

When statistics are allowed, the page creates a random visit number so the messages from one visit belong together. If you go from the homepage into the original Registrar console, the number is passed along once in that page's address and removed again straight away, so both pages count as one visit. The number is not stored on your device, and a new one is made when you come back or reload a page. It is never created if you choose NO or make no choice.

What is stored on your device

Only your choice: one entry in your browser's local storage for this website, named tc_observation, with the value granted or denied. It contains no identifier and no date. It lets both parts of the site follow your choice and keeps the question from appearing again. If your browser does not allow local storage, nothing is stored: statistics stay off, a YES applies only to the page you are on, and the question is asked again next time.

Where the messages go

If you choose YES, the messages are sent to a Google Apps Script web app on Google's servers (script.google.com), which the site owner set up to collect the results PENDING: where the results are stored (which spreadsheet service) and who can access them, to be confirmed by the site owner. As with any request over the internet, Google's servers receive your IP address and browser details in order to deliver each message; Google's own privacy policy describes how Google handles these.

Changing your mind

Use OBSERVATION PREFERENCES at the bottom of the homepage to switch statistics on or off at any time. Switching off stops all further messages immediately, on the homepage and in the original Registrar console. Clearing this website's data in your browser also removes your choice, and the question will be asked again.
