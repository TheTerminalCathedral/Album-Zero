What gets recorded?

Right now, nothing. Visitor statistics on this website are switched off. The site does not count page views, clicks or how long you stay, creates no visitor identifier, sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the hosting service receives the technical information your browser sends to load the pages, such as your IP address. GitHub's privacy statement describes how GitHub handles it.

Links to other websites (for example GitHub, DistroKid, Suno, LinkedIn, Reddit, Google Drive or ChatGPT) take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

If optional statistics are ever switched on, you will be asked first, and this page will describe exactly what they record.
