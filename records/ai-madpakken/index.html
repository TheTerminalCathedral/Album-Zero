AI Madpakken and the Human Sandwich Model

Created by:
Dan Almer Jensen
Form:
Written method, not software
License:
CC BY 4.0

AI Madpakken is an openly licensed workflow foundation for doing consequential work with AI. A human makes the decisions; AI works on both sides of them.

How the Human Sandwich Model works

COULD
An upstream AI model explores what could be done.
SHOULD
A human decides what should be done.
DID
A downstream executor does it, and reports what was actually done, with the evidence.

Reasoning can happen before and after the human. The authority to decide stays with the human.

What it is for

It is a practical answer to human-in-the-loop work with AI: who may decide what something means, how evidence is held to the claims it supports, and how a human stays the authority without becoming the bottleneck. Verification and research methods around the core are there to challenge confidence rather than add to it.

How it is used

The package is text. It is given to a model as its operating context and read before the model acts. Some of its documents are accepted method and others are marked EXPERIMENTAL; the labels are part of what they mean.

The Theme Park World project is one practical example.

Source

The canonical version is the main branch of TheTerminalCathedral/AI-Madpakken on GitHub, with its starting instructions and full license.

In the terminal, AI Madpakken is filed as a Direct Interface with the connection mode Full Plug-In. That is part of the Cathedral's fiction. There is no plug-in or software integration.
