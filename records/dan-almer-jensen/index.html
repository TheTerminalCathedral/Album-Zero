Dan Almer Jensen

Occupation:
Hardware Design Engineer
Profession:
Tech Polymath
Location:
Copenhagen, Denmark

Dan Almer Jensen trained as an Electronics Operator and an Electronics Technician, and works as a Hardware Design Engineer at Qutron A/S.

Professional background

Dan has worked with electronics for around 20 years, across a variety of industries:

  • safety electronics for railway systems
  • consumer audio research and development
  • military communications equipment
  • hardware development

Interests

Dan's interests rarely stay within one discipline: hardware design, troubleshooting, prototyping, programming, automation, music, and how AI can become a useful part of practical engineering.

Projects

  • AI Madpakken, an openly licensed workflow foundation built on the Human Sandwich Model
  • Theme Park World on Windows 11, an AI-assisted compatibility project
  • The Terminal Cathedral, an AI-assisted synthwave concept-album project
