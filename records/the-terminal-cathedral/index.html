The Terminal Cathedral: Album Zero and Album One

Created by:
Dan Almer Jensen
Form:
Synthwave concept albums
Albums:
Album Zero, Album One

The Terminal Cathedral is an AI-assisted synthwave concept-album project by Dan Almer Jensen about working with artificial intelligence through the Human Sandwich Model.

Where the universe came from

In Dan's own words:

When I started developing the story of The Terminal Cathedral, I needed a place for that story to unfold. So I began building a universe around it.

What started as a setting gradually grew into an extensive world of its own, with a lot of lore and worldbuilding — probably far more detailed and elaborate than two synthwave albums ever needed.

It's still a work in progress, and I keep finding new corners of it to explore.

The albums are one way to experience that universe, while also reflecting my own experiences of working with AI through the Human Sandwich Model.

Album Zero: the experiment

For Album Zero, I generated hundreds of songs using Suno. I explored the material, selected what worked, made simple edits and gradually assembled the first musical experience of The Terminal Cathedral.

It was a messy, experimental process, full of exploration and unexpected discoveries.

Listen to Album Zero

Album One: the refinement

With Album One, I took a more deliberate approach. I still used Suno, but worked in Suno Studio to shape the production more directly and have more control over the final result.

Listen to Album One

The human part

I also play guitar myself. It's one way I bring something directly human into the AI-assisted creative process.

Two albums. Two approaches to working with AI. One evolving experience of The Terminal Cathedral.
